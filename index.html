// About

Close to eight years building governed, compliant NHS workforce operations.

I'm a Chartered MCIPD workforce leader with a career spanning three London NHS trusts — West London NHS Trust, The Hillingdon Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust, and now Barking, Havering and Redbridge University Hospitals (BHRUT).

At BHRUT I run the Temporary Staffing and Medical Workforce Hub across Queen's Hospital and King George Hospital — overseeing bank and agency governance, e-rostering and compliance for the Trust's largest resident doctor cohort.

Outside the day-to-day, I'm drawn to the bigger questions in NHS workforce policy — how staffing data can close inequalities, and how digital transformation changes the way trusts are run.
